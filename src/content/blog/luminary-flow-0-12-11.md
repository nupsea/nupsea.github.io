## FLOW - Experience the Library through Luminary. 


### Determination

You've been procrastinating about reading. Your super talented friend inspires you.  Today, you decide to take a membership to the state library. 
> Some of the best things in life are free

(Install the MacOS Desktop Bundle). You have taken the first steps towards reading. You set up a reading time and make a timetable to read. 

<img src="/blog/luminary-flow-0-12-11/asset1.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:480px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />


### The Road Ahead

Its time to head towards the library. (start the Luminary MacOS app). You are accompanied by your friend and you both walk the green twisty pathways across the park to reach the library.

<img src="/blog/luminary-flow-0-12-11/asset2.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:480px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />

 You are greeted by huge doors of wooden structure to a magneficient campus. You enter and a quote by *"Socrates"* carved on a stone wall grabs your attention.
 > The unexamined life is not worth living

 
<img src="/blog/luminary-flow-0-12-11/asset3.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:480px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />

 You dwell on the thought and you are momentarily lost, until you get a message on your phone from your partner to meet her for lunch. You message back and put your phone on silent. You lift up your head to see the abundance of books at your disposal. You do not know what to start with. You decide on a genre, climb the winding stairs onto level 2 and go the section of your interest. You see the rack of books  (Luminary Libray page)


### The Scout

<img src="/blog/luminary-flow-0-12-11/asset4.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:800px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />
You select a book of your liking (click a Library Tile) and settle at a corner desk which has ambient window light and view towards the fountain. 

You open the book (Retrieval) and look at its title, look at the preface (Insights on the right pane) and Contents (Contents on the left pane) to get a feel of what the book is about (Insights : Key Points, Detailed, References, Tags). 

Before you begin with the book, you take a look at your watch and set a reminder for lunch with your partner. (start a pomodoro focus timer on top). 

<img src="/blog/luminary-flow-0-12-11/asset5.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:800px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />


As you do, you remove your pen, notebook, references, plain cards from your bag. You drink a sip of water, quench your thirst ..

 The smell of the paper entices you (paper mode in Universal Reader).  You grab your Notes and pen, and start writing your comprehension, draw your explanation and cement your understanding. (Open Notes to Write with Markdown support) 

<img src="/blog/luminary-flow-0-12-11/asset6.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:480px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />





As you tread along, you go into focussed mode of reading. 


<img src="/blog/luminary-flow-0-12-11/asset7.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:800px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />




### Dig Deep


You are not clear on a particular topic, even after re-reading. You ask your expert friend about it. He elaborates and gives his viewpoint (Select Text, Right Click -> Explain). 



<img src="/blog/luminary-flow-0-12-11/asset8.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:800px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />

You dicuss more and then your friend provides an analogy, and when you ask a deeper question he then prompts you to refer the book (Ask AI)

<img src="/blog/luminary-flow-0-12-11/asset9.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:800px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />








### Recall

Your reminder nudges you softly (Focus Timer Ends). Its about time to leave and meet your waiting partner. You let your friend know that its time to leave and pick up your stuff. You are glad, that you almost made to the end of the chapter. Your friend checks on you as to how well did you understand the topic. You are pretty confident and you ask him to throw some questions to know where you stand. On the way, to the restaurant he puts few questions across. Some of them are easy, some of them make you think..  Your neurons start firing and making strong connections..  

<img src="/blog/luminary-flow-0-12-11/asset10.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:480px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />



Anyway, now you have a good hold on yourself as to where you are.

### Sense of Satisfaction

You've reached the place, without realizing and being involved in the discussion and to see your smiling partner at a distance. Your partner greets you both, but your friend excuses himself and says goodbye (Luminary shuts down, Laptop closes - low on battery).

As you settle yourself with your partner at the table for lunch, you have a relaxed enoyable meal, laughing, talking and sharing. You are about to pay your bill, your partner notices a card fallen a metre from the table. She grabs and hands it to you with a smile. You have a look and a similar one shines on your face. (About)
