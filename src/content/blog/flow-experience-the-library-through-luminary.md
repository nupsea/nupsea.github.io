This is the experience I want reading with Luminary to feel like — not a feature list: a trip to the library. This release is a step toward that.

## Determination

You've been procrastinating about reading. Your super-talented friend inspires you. Today, you decide to take up a membership at the state library.

> *Some of the best things in life are free.*

*(Install the macOS Desktop Bundle.)* You've taken the first step toward reading. You set a reading time and put together a timetable.

## The Road Ahead

It's time to head to the library. *(Start the Luminary macOS app.)* You're accompanied by your friend, and together you walk the green, twisting pathway across the park to reach it.

<img src="/blog/flow-experience-the-library-through-luminary/asset2.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:480px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />

You're greeted by huge wooden doors opening onto a magnificent campus. As you step in, a quote carved into a stone wall catches your eye.

> *The unexamined life is not worth living.*
> — Socrates

<img src="/blog/flow-experience-the-library-through-luminary/asset3.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:480px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />

You linger on the thought, momentarily lost in it, until a message on your phone breaks the spell — your partner, asking you to meet for lunch. You reply and put your phone on silent. Looking up, you take in the sheer abundance of books before you. You don't know where to start. You settle on a genre, climb the winding stairs to level two, and head toward the section that interests you. You see the racks of books. *(The Luminary Library page.)*

## The Scout

<img src="/blog/flow-experience-the-library-through-luminary/asset4.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:800px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />

You pick a book that catches your eye *(click a Library tile)* and settle at a corner desk — ambient window light, a view toward the fountain.

You open the book *(Retrieval)*, glance at its title, skim the preface *(Insights, on the right pane)* and the contents *(Contents, on the left pane)* to get a feel for what it's about.

*(The preface breaks down into Key Points, Detailed, References, and Tags.)*

Before you begin, you check your watch and set a reminder for lunch with your partner. *(Start a Pomodoro focus timer, top of the screen.)*

<img src="/blog/flow-experience-the-library-through-luminary/asset5.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:800px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />

As you settle in, you take out your pen, notebook, reference cards. You sip some water, quench your thirst..

The smell of the paper draws you in *(paper mode in the Universal Reader)*. You grab your notebook and pen, and start writing down your understanding, sketching things out to cement what you've learned. *(Open Notes to write, with Markdown support.)*

<img src="/blog/flow-experience-the-library-through-luminary/asset6.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:480px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />

As you read on, you slip into a focused rhythm.

<img src="/blog/flow-experience-the-library-through-luminary/asset7.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:800px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />

## Dig Deep

A particular topic still isn't clicking, even after re-reading. You turn to your expert friend, who elaborates and shares his take. *(Select text, right-click → Explain.)*

<img src="/blog/flow-experience-the-library-through-luminary/asset8.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:800px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />

You discuss it further; your friend offers an analogy, and when you push with a deeper question, he points you back to the book itself. *(Ask AI.)*

<img src="/blog/flow-experience-the-library-through-luminary/asset9.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:800px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />

## Recall

Your reminder nudges you softly. *(Focus Timer ends.)* It's about time to leave and meet your waiting partner. You let your friend know it's time to go and gather your things — glad you nearly made it to the end of the chapter. Your friend checks in: how well did you really understand the topic? Feeling fairly confident, you ask him to quiz you. On the way to the restaurant, he throws a few questions your way — some easy, some that make you think. Your neurons start firing, forging stronger connections.

<img src="/blog/flow-experience-the-library-through-luminary/asset10.png" alt="Pasted Image" style="display:block;max-width:480px;width:100%;margin:1.5rem auto;border-radius:0.5rem;" />

Either way, you now have a good, clear hold on where you stand.

## Sense of Satisfaction

Caught up in the discussion, you barely notice arriving — until you spot your partner smiling in the distance. She greets you both, but your friend excuses himself and says goodbye. *(Luminary shuts down — laptop closing, low on battery.)*

As you settle in with your partner for lunch, you have a relaxed, enjoyable meal — laughing, talking, sharing. Just as you're about to pay the bill, your partner notices a card fallen a couple of feet from the table. She picks it up and hands it to you with a smile. You take a look, and a similar smile spreads across your face. *(About.)*
